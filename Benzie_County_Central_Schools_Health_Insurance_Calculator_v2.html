Methodology & notes
• Employee annual costs are the 2027 caps-adjusted employee contributions from The Pool Benzie County Central Schools rate sheet (1/1/2027). The district pays the 2027 PA 152 hard cap ($8,180.35 Single / $17,107.66 Two-Person / $22,310.11 Family per year); the employee pays the rest. Per-pay deductions are the annual cost divided by the number of pays selected above.
• Where the plan premium is below the hard cap, the employee cost is $0.00 and the difference is deposited into the employee's HSA, shown as a negative amount. Under the 2027 rates, all four plans carry an employee cost at every coverage level, so no HSA deposit applies.
• Benefits are from the BCBSM Benefits-at-a-Glance documents (Enhanced HSA 2000 121/122 effective 1/1/2025; Enhanced HSA 2500 210/211 effective 1/1/2026; Enhanced HSA Level 119/120 effective 1/1/2027) and the Priority Health HSA 2500 PH01 plan design shown on the Benzie County Central Schools 2027 rate sheet.
• The medical slider (Checkups → Major) sets office, online, urgent care and ER visits plus hospital/procedure charges per person; the Rx slider (None → Specialty) sets generic, preferred brand/specialty and non-preferred fills per person. Two-Person is modeled as 2 people and Full Family as 4. Typical charges: primary care $150, specialist $250, online visit $75, urgent care $350, ER $2,000. All four plans are HSA-qualified, so the full charge goes to the deductible, then coinsurance (0% on 121/122, 210/211 and PH01; 20% on 119/120) up to the out-of-pocket maximum. Hospital/procedure charges are treated the same way.
• Rx (30-day retail) is modeled at the stated copay per fill after the deductible, with no multiplier: Enhanced HSA 2000 121/122 and Enhanced HSA Level 119/120 – $10 / $80 / $100; Enhanced HSA 2500 210/211 – $20 / $80 / $100; Priority HSA 2500 PH01 – $10 / $80 / $160. Where the plan uses 20% brand coinsurance, the fill is modeled at the stated per-fill maximum, since 20% of a typical brand-name drug cost reaches that maximum. Rx dollars paid also count toward the deductible and out-of-pocket maximum.
• Two-person and family contracts use family deductible, coinsurance and out-of-pocket amounts applied to total household usage (all four plans use aggregate family deductibles). Rx counts toward the out-of-pocket maximum. Preventive care is covered at 100% and is not included.
